Salt Creek Midstream, LLC On-Call Alarm Text Messaging Program

SMS Terms

Program name: Salt Creek Midstream, LLC On-Call Alarm Notifications

Description: Salt Creek Midstream, LLC sends automated text messages to on-call employees on company-issued mobile phones when a monitored alarm requires attention. Messages are operational alerts only. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

Enrollment: Receiving alarm text messages is a required part of on-call duty. Employees acknowledge and consent in writing using the On-Call Alarm Text Message Acknowledgment and Consent form before receiving messages.

Message frequency: Message frequency varies with operating conditions.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out: Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. After STOP, no further alarm text messages will be sent to that number unless the employee replies START or UNSTOP.

Help: Reply HELP for help, or contact scm-it@scmid.com.

Carrier liability: Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy: Participation in this program is subject to our Privacy Policy: https://sms.saltcreekmidstream.com/privacy.
